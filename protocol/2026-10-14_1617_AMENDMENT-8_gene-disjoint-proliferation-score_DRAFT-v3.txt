WHY THIS AMENDMENT
  The 18-gene score (P2) consists of PAM50 proliferation genes, and MKI67 (P1) is itself a PAM50
  gene. Agreement of either score with PAM50 is therefore partly built in. The question is whether
  the main pattern (kappa-optimal percentile tracks 100 minus the luminal B share; the Youden
  boundary stays fixed on the luminal A quantile scale) depends on that overlap. A score that
  shares no gene with PAM50 answers this. Lower peak agreement is expected and is not itself a
  test of the pattern. The pathologist-scored Ki-67 test of Amendment 7 measures protein and is
  unaffected by this limitation.

PRE-SPECIFIED PREDICTIONS FOR P3 (CONFIRMATORY FOR THIS AMENDMENT)
  G1  In each of TCGA-BRCA, METABRIC and SCAN-B, the observed kappa-optimal percentile of P3 lies
      within 5 points of 100 minus the observed luminal B share.
  G2  Resampling (design of Amendment 6, E3: 200 resamples per luminal B share of 15, 20, 25, 30,
      35, 40, 45% within the luminal set, class-conditional distributions unchanged): in each of
      the three cohorts, the slope of the median kappa-optimal percentile of P3 on (100 - share)
      lies in [0.7, 1.3].
  G3  Same resampling: in each of the three cohorts, the slope of the median Youden-optimal
      cut-off of P3, on the luminal A quantile scale, on (100 - share) lies in [-0.3, 0.3].
  G4  In GSE81538, the observed kappa-optimal percentile of P3 lies within 5 points of 100 minus
      the observed luminal B share (36.6%, so 63.4).
  Decision: the pattern is CONFIRMED FOR A GENE-DISJOINT SCORE if G1, G2 and G3 all hold. It is
  NOT CONFIRMED if any of them fails; the result is then reported as such, with the cohort and
  criterion that failed. G4 is reported as a supporting test and does not alter the decision.
  Tolerances repeat those already registered for P2 so that the two scores are judged alike.

PRE-SPECIFIED DESCRIPTIVE ANALYSES
  H1  Peak kappa with PAM50 for P1, P2, P3, P3m and P4 at their own optima, per cohort, with
      2,000-resample bootstrap CIs. This quantifies how much agreement the shared genes supplied.
  H2  Spearman correlation of P3, P3m and P4 with P2 within the ER-positive, HER2-negative stratum
      and within luminal A and luminal B separately.
  H3  G1 to G3 repeated for P3m and P4, judged by the same rules and reported as secondary.
  H4  Youden boundary of P3 on the luminal A quantile scale per cohort, with bootstrap CIs.
  P1 and P2 are rerun through the same code so that all five scores are handled identically.
  Random seed 20261014. Analysis code: code/s21_amendment8.py (mode "run"), which refuses to start
  unless both list files carry FROZEN in their names.

WHAT WILL NOT BE DONE
  No survival analysis with P3 or P4. No removal of individual genes from P3 or P4 after
  expression is seen (the erythroid and megakaryocyte members flagged in the P3 gene-list file stay
  in). No change to the main analysis or to registered results, including the Amendment 7 verdict
  (not confirmed on IHC). No new reference standard.

LIMITS STATED IN ADVANCE
  PAM50 remains the reference, so P3 removes shared genes but not shared biology: the PAM50
  luminal A/B split is itself driven by proliferation, so some agreement is expected for any valid
  proliferation measure. P3 was derived in normal tissue and was not designed for breast cancer,
  so its separation of luminal A from B may be weaker, which widens intervals. P4 contains genes
  outside proliferation (for example HIF1A, NOTCH2, EGF, MEIS1, SMAD3, TGFB1).
