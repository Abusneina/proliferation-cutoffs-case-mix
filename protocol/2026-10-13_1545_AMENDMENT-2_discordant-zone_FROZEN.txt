PROTOCOL AMENDMENT 2  -  FROZEN PRE-SPECIFICATION
Study: Sensitivity of surrogate luminal A vs luminal B assignment to the proliferation cut-off
Frozen: 2026-10-13 15:45 UTC+2, BEFORE any outcome comparison between discordant-zone groups.
Author: Abdelmuhsen M. Abusneina

STATUS OF WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN AT FREEZE TIME
  Analyses A1-A6 are complete. Their results are known and are listed here so that it is
  explicit which quantities informed this amendment:
    - kappa-optimal percentile, P2: TCGA 74th, METABRIC 59th
    - kappa-optimal percentile, P1: TCGA 77th, METABRIC 64th
    - luminal-B prevalence at own optimum: TCGA 26%, METABRIC 41%
    - transfer loss (percentile) 0.08-0.09 kappa; (absolute) 0.14-0.17 kappa
  NO outcome comparison involving a discordant group has been computed. The group
  definition below is derived from agreement with PAM50 only, never from survival.

RATIONALE
  A1-A6 establish that the optimal cut-off is cohort-specific. That is a statement about
  classification. It does not establish whether the disagreement matters prognostically.
  If the band of proliferation values over which cohorts disagree carries no prognostic
  information, then the choice of threshold inside that band is arbitrary in the only
  sense that matters clinically, because the luminal A/B split governs the adjuvant
  chemotherapy recommendation.

GROUP DEFINITION (fixed, derived from A3 agreement only)
  For each cohort and each score S, let c_low and c_high be the LOWER and HIGHER of the
  two cohort-specific kappa-optimal percentiles established in A3, computed within that
  cohort's own ER-positive/HER2-negative stratum:
      P2: c_low = 59th percentile, c_high = 74th percentile
      P1: c_low = 64th percentile, c_high = 77th percentile
  Each patient is assigned to exactly one group:
      STABLE_A     S <= c_low        luminal A-like under either threshold
      DISCORDANT   c_low < S <= c_high   label depends on which threshold is used
      STABLE_B     S >  c_high       luminal B-like under either threshold

PRE-SPECIFIED ANALYSES
  D1  Three-group Cox model, DISCORDANT as the reference category, adjusted for age and
      stage, in each cohort and for each score. Report HR and 95% CI for STABLE_A vs
      DISCORDANT and STABLE_B vs DISCORDANT.
  D2  Equivalence assessment. Absence of significance is not evidence of absence, so the
      confidence interval is the quantity reported, not the p-value. Equivalence margin
      fixed here at HR within [0.67, 1.50].
  D3  THE DECISIVE TEST. Within the DISCORDANT group only, fit a Cox model with the
      CONTINUOUS score S as the predictor, adjusted for age and stage. If the continuous
      score does not predict outcome inside the zone, then no threshold placed inside the
      zone can separate prognosis, and the zone is uninformative by construction.
  D4  Trend test across the three ordered groups, to establish whether the relationship is
      monotone or flat in the middle.
  D5  Clinical translation. Report the number and percentage of the ER-positive,
      HER2-negative cohort falling in DISCORDANT, i.e. the proportion of patients whose
      adjuvant chemotherapy recommendation depends on which threshold the laboratory used.

DECISION RULES, FIXED BEFORE EXECUTION
  R1  The discordant zone is declared PROGNOSTICALLY EMPTY only if ALL of the following
      hold, in BOTH cohorts, for BOTH scores:
        (a) D3 continuous-score coefficient inside the zone has p > 0.05, AND
        (b) the D3 hazard ratio per standard deviation has a 95% CI contained within
            the equivalence margin [0.67, 1.50].
      Criterion (b) is required so that a null is claimed only when the data are precise
      enough to exclude a clinically meaningful effect.
  R2  If D3 is significant in any cohort, the zone is NOT empty, and the finding is
      reported as the opposite: the discordant band carries prognostic information and the
      threshold placement within it is consequential. This outcome is publishable and will
      be reported with equal prominence.
  R3  A result present in one cohort only is reported as cohort-specific, never as replicated.
  R4  Multiplicity: D1, D3 and D4 constitute one family per cohort; Benjamini-Hochberg at
      FDR 0.05 within cohort.
  R5  No alternative definition of c_low or c_high will be substituted after results are
      seen. Sensitivity analyses using other spans may be added only as explicitly labelled
      post hoc exploration, never as a replacement for the above.

ANTICIPATED LIMITATION, STATED IN ADVANCE
  The DISCORDANT group is by construction intermediate in proliferation, so an intermediate
  hazard is the expected result and is not itself informative. The informative quantity is
  D3: whether proliferation predicts outcome WITHIN the band. This is stated now so that an
  intermediate hazard is not later presented as though it were the finding.
