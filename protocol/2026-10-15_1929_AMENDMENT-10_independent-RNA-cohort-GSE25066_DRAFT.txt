WHY THIS AMENDMENT
  The main pattern was first seen in three cohorts and was then tested in GSE81538, which comes from
  the same initiative as SCAN-B. A cohort from a different population, country, platform and
  clinical setting tests whether the pattern transfers. The prediction is the one already
  registered: the kappa-optimal cut-off sits near 100 minus the luminal B share and moves with it
  under resampling, while the Youden boundary on the luminal A quantile scale stays fixed.

ELIGIBILITY
  GSE25066: ER positive by the field "er_status_ihc_esr1_for indeterminate" = P (IHC, with ESR1
    mRNA used where IHC was indeterminate, as deposited); HER2 negative by her2_status = N
    (I and NA excluded); pam50_class present. Fields are parsed by field name for each sample,
    because their order differs between samples.
  SMC 2018: IHC_SUBTYPE = ER+ (ER+HER2+, HER2+, TN and UA excluded); PAM50_SUBTYPE present
    (NA excluded); expression available.
  Agreement analyses use PAM50 luminal A and luminal B tumors; percentiles are taken over the whole
  eligible stratum, as in Amendments 5, 6 and 8.

EXPRESSION AND SCORES
  GSE25066: values log2(max(x, 1)). Probes annotated to exactly one gene symbol in GPL96.annot are
    kept (probes mapped to several genes are dropped). For each gene, the probe with the highest
    mean log2 value across all 508 samples is used; this is computed once, before eligibility is
    applied, and uses no subtype information. Legacy symbols are resolved with the Amendment 8
    alias table.
  SMC 2018: values log2(TPM + 1).
  Each gene is standardized within the eligible stratum of its own cohort.
  P1  MKI67.
  P2  Mean of the standardized PAM50 proliferation genes available on the platform. GSE25066 has
      15 of 18 (ANLN, NUF2 and UBE2T have no probe on U133A); SMC 2018 has all 18. The reduced
      set is stated in every report.
  P3  Median of the standardized Amendment 8 gene-disjoint genes (frozen list
      2026-10-14_1641_GENE-LIST_P3-metaPCNA-minus-PAM50_FROZEN.txt). GSE25066 has 118 of 120 with
      single-gene probes; SMC 2018 has 120.
  P4  As in Amendment 8, descriptive only (GSE25066 182 of 187; SMC 2018 187 of 187).

PRE-SPECIFIED PREDICTIONS (GSE25066; P2 and P3 co-primary)
  For each of P2 and P3:
  R1  The observed kappa-optimal percentile lies within 5 points of 100 minus the observed
      luminal B share.
  R2  Resampling as in Amendment 6 (200 resamples at luminal B shares of 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40 and
      45% within the luminal set, class-conditional distributions unchanged): the slope of the
      median kappa-optimal percentile on (100 - share) lies in [0.7, 1.3].
  R3  Same resampling: the slope of the median Youden cut-off on the luminal A quantile scale lies
      in [-0.3, 0.3].
  R4  (supporting) The 2,000-resample bootstrap interval of the Youden boundary on the luminal A
      quantile scale overlaps the 82nd to 85th quantile.
  Decision: CONFIRMED IN AN INDEPENDENT COHORT if R1, R2 and R3 all hold for both P2 and P3.
  NOT CONFIRMED if any of them fails for either score; the score and criterion that failed are
  reported. R4 is reported and does not change the decision.
  If fewer than 60 tumors of either luminal subtype are eligible, the test is reported as
  underpowered and the decision is stated as "not tested"; this threshold is fixed here because the
  subtype counts are unknown.

SECONDARY (SMC 2018; descriptive, no effect on the decision)
  R1 to R4 for P2 and P3, with the same code. Reported with the number of eligible tumors.

DESCRIPTIVE
  Peak kappa with 2,000-resample bootstrap intervals for P1 to P4; Spearman correlation of P3 with
  P2 within the eligible stratum.

WHAT WILL NOT BE DONE
  No survival analysis. No re-derivation of PAM50 calls; the deposited calls are the reference.
  No change to the probe rule, eligibility or criteria after data are read. No change to any
  registered result.

LIMITS STATED IN ADVANCE
  GSE25066 consists of core-needle or fine-needle pretreatment biopsies from patients selected for
  neoadjuvant chemotherapy, so its case mix differs from the surgical cohorts; this is part of what
  the test examines. Its PAM50 calls come from an Affymetrix implementation of the centroid
  classifier, which differs from the implementations in the other cohorts. P2 lacks three genes.
  SMC 2018 is small and drawn mostly from young Korean women.
