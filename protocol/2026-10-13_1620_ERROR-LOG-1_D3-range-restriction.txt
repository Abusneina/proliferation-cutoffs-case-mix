ERROR LOG 1  -  specification error in Amendment 2, analysis D3
Recorded: 2026-10-13 16:20 UTC+2, after D3 was executed and before any write-up.

WHAT WAS SPECIFIED
  D3 fitted the continuous proliferation score inside the discordant zone, standardized
  WITHIN the zone, and R1(b) required its HR per SD to fall inside [0.67, 1.50].

WHY THIS IS INVALID
  The zone spans only 0.35-0.44 whole-cohort SD. Standardizing inside it makes one unit
  equal to about 0.11 whole-cohort SD. If the whole-cohort slope (HR 1.52 per SD, METABRIC
  P2) held perfectly inside the zone, the expected HR per within-zone unit is
  1.52^0.11 = 1.05, which lies inside the margin by construction. Criterion R1(b) was
  therefore satisfied regardless of biology. The D3 "null" is range restriction, not
  evidence of a flat region.

EVIDENCE (whole-cohort SD units)
  METABRIC P2 inside zone: HR 0.40, 95% CI 0.05-2.99   (uninformative)
  METABRIC P1 inside zone: HR 0.68, 95% CI 0.10-4.85   (uninformative)
  TCGA, both scores: CIs spanning more than two orders of magnitude
  Expected HR across the full zone width under the whole-cohort slope: 1.11-1.17

CONSEQUENCE
  The claim "the discordant zone is prognostically empty" is WITHDRAWN. It is not
  supported and was never testable by a within-zone slope with these sample sizes.
  Figure 2 of 2026-10-13_1600 (whole cohort vs inside zone, per SD) is withdrawn.

WHAT REMAINS VALID (unaffected by the scaling error)
  D1 group contrasts, D4 trend, D5 zone size. In METABRIC the discordant group is worse
  than stable luminal A-like and not distinguishable from stable luminal B-like.

HOW IT WAS FOUND
  A descriptive post hoc plot of hazard by twentieth of the score (Figure S1) did not show
  the flat band the D3 result implied, which prompted the unit check.
