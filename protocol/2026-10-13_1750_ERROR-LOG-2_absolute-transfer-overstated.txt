ERROR LOG 2  -  interpretation error in A5 ("transport the rank, not the number")
Recorded: 2026-10-13 17:50 UTC+2.

WHAT WAS CLAIMED (chat summary of 13 Oct, not yet in any manuscript text)
  Transferring a threshold as an absolute value costs about twice as much agreement as
  transferring it as a percentile, so laboratories should "transport the rank, not the number".

WHY IT IS OVERSTATED
  The "absolute" arm of A5 transferred the threshold on the within-stratum z-score scale,
  which is itself a relative scale. Raw expression cannot be transferred between RNA-seq
  (TCGA RSEM, SCAN-B FPKM) and microarray (METABRIC) at all. The z-versus-percentile gap
  therefore measures differences in distribution SHAPE between cohorts (e.g. MKI67 skew
  -0.47 in TCGA vs +0.89 in METABRIC), not the cost of carrying a raw value between labs.

WHAT REMAINS TRUE
  Across all 8 transfer comparisons (TCGA<->METABRIC and both into SCAN-B), the z-scale
  transfer lost at least as much agreement as the percentile transfer. Reportable as:
  "distribution shape differs between cohorts, so even a standardized threshold does not
  carry over". The laboratory-practice recommendation is withdrawn.
