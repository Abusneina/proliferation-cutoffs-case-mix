PROTOCOL AMENDMENT 9  -  OVERALL SURVIVAL AS A COMMON ENDPOINT (LIMITATION: DIFFERING ENDPOINTS)  -  DRAFT
Drafted: 2026-10-14 17:10 UTC+2. Author: Abdelmuhsen M. Abusneina.
Status: DRAFT. Becomes binding only when renamed FROZEN, time-stamped, and posted on OSF
(osf.io/cwkd9) BEFORE any overall-survival comparison between cut-off groups is computed in
TCGA-BRCA or METABRIC, and before the in-zone analysis (D3) is computed in SCAN-B.

WHY THIS AMENDMENT
  The discordant-group outcome analyses used different endpoints: progression-free interval in
  TCGA-BRCA, disease-specific survival derived from vital status in METABRIC (Amendment 2), and
  overall survival in SCAN-B, the only outcome released there (Amendments 3 and 4). The pooled
  estimates of Amendment 4 therefore combine different quantities. Overall survival is released
  in all three cohorts. This amendment repeats the registered group comparisons with overall
  survival everywhere, so that the cohorts can be compared and pooled on one endpoint.

DISCLOSED PRIOR KNOWLEDGE (results already known at drafting)
  - Amendment 2 (D1 to D5) in TCGA-BRCA (progression-free interval) and METABRIC (derived
    disease-specific survival), both scores. METABRIC P2: STABLE_A vs DISCORDANT HR 0.66
    (0.50 to 0.86); STABLE_B vs DISCORDANT HR 1.23 (0.92 to 1.63). TCGA-BRCA: 12 events in the
    discordant group.
  - Amendments 3 and 4 in SCAN-B (overall survival), both scores, primary and grade-adjusted.
    P2: STABLE_A vs DISCORDANT HR 0.77 (0.53 to 1.14); STABLE_B vs DISCORDANT HR 1.20 (0.80 to
    1.81). These SCAN-B C1 estimates already use overall survival and are carried into this
    amendment unchanged; they are not re-estimated.
  - Field availability only: OS_STATUS and OS_MONTHS are complete for all 573 TCGA-BRCA and all
    1,397 METABRIC patients analyzed. No overall-survival event count by group, and no
    overall-survival model, has been computed in either cohort.
  - Code test (s22_amendment9.py, mode "dryrun", 2026-10-14 17:15): outcomes were randomly
    permuted within each cohort before any model was fitted, so no real group-outcome
    association was computed. The test revealed only cohort totals of overall-survival deaths:
    TCGA-BRCA 54 of 573, METABRIC 811 of 1,397, SCAN-B 217 of 2,425 (the last already known).
    METABRIC (58% deaths, long follow-up) will dominate the pooled estimate, and many of its
    deaths are from other causes.
  - The METABRIC disease-specific events are a subset of its overall-survival events (deaths
    from breast cancer within all deaths), and the follow-up time is the same. The METABRIC
    overall-survival result is therefore partly predictable from the known result and is not
    independent evidence.
  - The in-zone continuous analysis (D3) has never been run in SCAN-B.

WHAT IS NEW
  (i) TCGA-BRCA and METABRIC with overall survival, analyses D1, D3, D4 and D5 of Amendment 2.
  (ii) SCAN-B analyses D3 and D4 (overall survival). D1 in SCAN-B is the registered C1 result.
  (iii) Pooled estimates on a single endpoint.

DESIGN (all elements as already registered; only the endpoint changes)
  Patients, scores (P1, P2), percentile cut-offs (P2: 59th and 74th; P1: 64th and 77th, computed
  within each cohort's ER-positive, HER2-negative stratum), group definitions (STABLE_A,
  DISCORDANT, STABLE_B), Cox models, equivalence margin [0.67, 1.50], and covariate-dropping rule
  exactly as in Amendment 2 (TCGA-BRCA, METABRIC) and Amendment 4 (SCAN-B).
  Endpoint: overall survival, death from any cause.
    TCGA-BRCA  OS_MONTHS, event = OS_STATUS "1:DECEASED".
    METABRIC   OS_MONTHS, event = OS_STATUS "1:DECEASED".
    SCAN-B     overall survival days / 30.4375, event = overall survival event (as registered).
  Adjustment: age and stage group (TCGA-BRCA, METABRIC); age, tumor size and nodal group
  (SCAN-B). Grade-adjusted models in SCAN-B as a sensitivity analysis, as registered.
  P2 primary, P1 reported alongside.

PRE-SPECIFIED ANALYSES
  O1  Per cohort, D1: STABLE_A vs DISCORDANT and STABLE_B vs DISCORDANT, HR and 95% CI.
  O2  Pooled estimate on overall survival, inverse-variance fixed effect on the adjusted log
      hazard ratios, one estimate per cohort, for STABLE_A vs DISCORDANT (C1a) and STABLE_B vs
      DISCORDANT (C1b).
        Primary pooled set: TCGA-BRCA + METABRIC + SCAN-B.
        Sensitivity: METABRIC + SCAN-B; DerSimonian-Laird random effects with I-squared.
  O3  D3 in each cohort: continuous score (standardized within the discordant group) inside the
      discordant group, adjusted as above, with the D3 model in the whole cohort for contrast.
  O4  D4 trend across the three ordered groups in each cohort.
  O5  D5 number and percentage in the discordant group and overall-survival events by group.

DECISION RULES
  For pooled C1b (primary set, P2), as in Amendments 3 and 4:
    CI inside [0.67, 1.50]                    -> "B-like supported on a common endpoint"
    CI includes 1 but extends beyond margin   -> "inconclusive"
    CI excludes 1                             -> "refuted" (the discordant group differs from
                                                 stable luminal B-like)
  For pooled C1a (primary set, P2): reported as HR and CI; a CI wholly below 1 is read as
    "discordant group has worse overall survival than stable luminal A-like".
  For O3, rule R1 of Amendment 2 applies per cohort (p > 0.05 AND CI within [0.67, 1.50] for a
    cohort to be called prognostically empty in the zone); R2 and R3 apply unchanged.
  Multiplicity: Benjamini-Hochberg at FDR 0.05 within cohort over O1, O3 and O4, as in R4.

STATUS OF THE RESULTS
  Overall survival is a secondary endpoint. The registered per-cohort results with their
  original endpoints remain the primary outcome evidence and are not replaced. Where the two
  disagree, both are reported. Overall survival includes deaths from other causes, which dilutes
  contrasts that act through breast cancer; this is stated in advance, and an attenuated
  overall-survival hazard ratio is not read as evidence against the primary result. TCGA-BRCA
  is expected to contribute few events and little weight. No survival analysis with P3 or P4
  (Amendment 8) is done.

WHAT WILL NOT BE DONE
  No change to cut-offs, group definitions, covariates, margin or pooling method. No
  cause-specific or competing-risks model is added after results are seen. No endpoint other
  than overall survival is introduced.
