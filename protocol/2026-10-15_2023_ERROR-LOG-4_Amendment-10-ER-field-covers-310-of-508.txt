ERROR LOG 4  -  AMENDMENT 10: THE CHOSEN ER FIELD EXISTS FOR ONLY 310 OF 508 GSE25066 SAMPLES
Recorded: 2026-10-15 20:23 UTC+2 (time read from the system clock), after the Amendment 10 run (20:22) and
before any write-up. Author: Abdelmuhsen M. Abusneina.

WHAT WAS SPECIFIED
  Amendment 10 defined ER positivity in GSE25066 by the field "er_status_ihc_esr1_for indeterminate" = P.

WHAT WAS WRONG
  That field is present for 310 of the 508 samples (the discovery series, GSE25055). The field
  "er_status_ihc", present for all 508, was available and was not chosen. The non-missing count of
  310 was printed during the pre-freeze field listing (search log of 19:28) and was overlooked.
  The error was made in drafting; it was not caused by any data seen.

CONSEQUENCE
  The eligible ER-positive, HER2-negative stratum had 167 tumors, of which 93 were luminal A and 38
  luminal B. Under the rule fixed in Amendment 10 (at least 60 tumors in each luminal subtype),
  the registered verdict is NOT TESTED (underpowered). This verdict stands and is reported as such.

WHAT WILL BE DONE
  The registered result is not changed. Any analysis using "er_status_ihc" for all 508 samples is
  now post hoc, because the Amendment 10 results are known; if run, it will be labeled exploratory,
  will not produce a confirmatory verdict, and will be reported next to the registered result.
