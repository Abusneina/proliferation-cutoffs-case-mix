ERROR LOG 5  -  PERCENTILE SCALE OF THE RESAMPLING SLOPE; DENOMINATOR OF THE OBSERVED OPTIMA
Recorded: 2026-10-16 11:53 UTC+2 (time read from the system clock). Author: Abdelmuhsen M. Abusneina.
Updated: 2026-10-16 11:57 UTC+2 (clock): P3 and GSE81538 score-unit results added.
Found by: external review of manuscript v0.16, items 3 and 4; confirmed by post hoc check ph1_scale_check.py.

WHAT WAS WRONG
1. Scale. Amendment 6 (E3), Amendment 7 (C1), Amendment 8 and Amendment 10 measured movement of the
   kappa-optimal cut-off as the slope of its PERCENTILE within the resampled luminal set on 100 minus the
   imposed luminal B share. When the share changes, the percentile of a cut-off that does not move at all
   changes by F_A(c) minus F_B(c) per point of share (0.44 to 0.70 in the three main cohorts). The registered
   slope criterion (0.7 to 1.3) therefore mixed a scale component with real movement of the cut-off. The
   manuscript's statement that the optimum moved "about one point for every point" of share overstated
   movement in score units.
2. Youden invariance. Resampling within each subtype keeps sensitivity and specificity curves fixed, so the
   Youden cut-off cannot move except by sampling noise. Its slope near 0 (E3, C2) is a property of the
   design, not a finding.
3. Denominator. The observed kappa optima (74, 59, 71) were percentiles of the whole ER+/HER2- stratum and
   were compared with 100 minus the luminal B share computed within luminal tumors (73.9, 61.1, 70.5).

CONSEQUENCES APPLIED
- Registered results are reported unchanged, on their registered scale, with this log cited.
- Post hoc analysis (labelled as such): kappa-optimal cut-off in score units, expressed as a quantile of the
  full luminal A distribution, under the Amendment 6 design (seed 20261016). Slopes: 18-gene score 0.20
  (TCGA-BRCA), 0.37 (METABRIC), 0.22 (SCAN-B); MKI67 0.34, 0.57, 0.23. The slope a fixed cut-off would show
  on the percentile scale: 0.65, 0.67, 0.70 (18-gene); 0.53, 0.44, 0.62 (MKI67).
- Observed optima restated as percentiles of the luminal set: 74.5, 59.2, 73.3 (18-gene) against 73.9,
  61.1, 70.5; within 3 points.
- Ki-67 (Amendment 7): the kappa-optimal consensus Ki-67 threshold stayed at 15% at imposed shares of 25%
  to 45% (A7_resampling.csv), so the C1 slope of 0.62 reflects mainly the percentile scale.
- Same post hoc analysis for P3 (ph2_score_units_P3.py, Amendment 8 scoring functions unchanged, expression
  files re-downloaded with matching MD5): score-unit slopes 0.41 (TCGA-BRCA), 0.42 (METABRIC), 0.17 (SCAN-B),
  0.43 (GSE81538); P2 in GSE81538 0.28. P2 values reproduced ph1 exactly.
- The independent cohorts (GSE25066, SMC 2018) were not re-analysed in score units; their percentile slopes
  are read as direction only.
- Manuscript claims rewritten in v0.17.
