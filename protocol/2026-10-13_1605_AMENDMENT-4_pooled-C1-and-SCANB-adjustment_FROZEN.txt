1. WHY
   SCAN-B has 217 overall-survival events in 2,425 ER+/HER2- patients. The C1b contrast
   (STABLE_B vs DISCORDANT) is expected to rest on roughly 100 events, giving a 95% CI of
   about the width of the equivalence margin. A single-cohort C1b result is therefore likely
   to be inconclusive. A pooled estimate is pre-specified here, before groups exist in SCAN-B.

2. DISCLOSED PRIOR KNOWLEDGE
   The METABRIC inputs to the pooled estimate are already known (Amendment 2, D1):
     HR(STABLE_B vs DISCORDANT): P2 1.23 [0.92, 1.63]; P1 1.02 [0.76, 1.37]
     HR(STABLE_A vs DISCORDANT): P2 0.66 [0.50, 0.86]; P1 0.57 [0.43, 0.75]
   The only new information entering the pooled estimate is the SCAN-B result.

3. POOLED ANALYSIS
   Primary pooled set: METABRIC + SCAN-B.
   Method: inverse-variance fixed-effect pooling of adjusted log hazard ratios, one estimate
   per cohort, for C1a (STABLE_A vs DISCORDANT) and C1b (STABLE_B vs DISCORDANT), P2 primary,
   P1 reported alongside.
   Sensitivity: DerSimonian-Laird random effects with I-squared; and a three-cohort pool
   adding TCGA.
   Decision rule for pooled C1b is identical to Amendment 3: CI inside [0.67, 1.50] ->
   "B-like confirmed"; CI includes 1 but exceeds the margin -> "inconclusive"; CI excludes 1
   -> "refuted".

4. STATUS OF THE POOLED RESULT
   Per-cohort results remain the primary evidence and are reported first. The pooled
   estimate combines different endpoints (METABRIC derived disease-specific survival,
   SCAN-B overall survival, TCGA progression-free interval in the sensitivity pool) and is
   labelled as such wherever it appears. A pooled "B-like confirmed" with an "inconclusive"
   SCAN-B result is reported as pooled support, never as independent replication.

5. SCAN-B ADJUSTMENT SET (fixes Amendment 3, which left it conditional)
   Primary: age at diagnosis, tumor size (continuous, mm), lymph node group
   (NodeNegative / SubMicroMet / 1to3 / 4toX). Tumor size and nodal group are used because
   they are the components of stage, matching the stage adjustment in TCGA and METABRIC.
   Histological grade (NHG) is NOT adjusted in the primary model: NHG includes a mitotic
   count, which is itself a proliferation measure, so adjusting for it would absorb part of
   the exposure. A grade-adjusted model is reported as a sensitivity analysis only.

6. PROHIBITED
   No change to the pooled set, pooling method, margin, or adjustment set after SCAN-B
   groups are formed.
