OSF REGISTRATION UPDATE  -  for attachment to osf.io/pc9wm (project osf.io/cwkd9)
Systematic review: Does the luminal B share of a study population predict the Ki-67 cut-off it reports
as optimal? Protocol v1.0 (MD5 a16d2aedb9da0a8baf1213fdfb7283f4).
Date: 13 October 2026. Author: Abdelmuhsen M. Abusneina.

1. Feasibility outcome (protocol Section 5)
The protocol requires at least 8 studies in the primary stratum (prevalence-dependent derivation
criterion, PAM50 or intrinsic-subtype reference) before the primary meta-regression is run, with a
pre-specified fallback that pools the intrinsic-subtype and risk-assay reference classes, and a
descriptive report if fewer than 8 studies remain.

After the registered database search, full-text assessment, and backward and forward citation searching
of the included studies, the first-pass counts are:
  - primary stratum: 2 to 3 studies (one provisional, pending supplementary data);
  - pooled fallback stratum: 4 studies.
Both are below the threshold. As pre-specified, the primary test will not be run and the review will be
reported descriptively, with no inferential claim about the slope.

These counts come from a first-pass assessment and will be confirmed by a second, independent human
reviewer. If dual review changes the count to 8 or more, the registered analysis will be run as written.

2. Form of publication
The descriptive synthesis will be reported as a pre-registered component of a primary research article
on the same question in three public cohorts (TCGA-BRCA, METABRIC, SCAN-B). The registration, this note
and Deviation 1 (search run before registration) will be cited and disclosed in that article.

3. Changes to the protocol
None. No eligibility criterion, outcome or analysis rule has been changed.
