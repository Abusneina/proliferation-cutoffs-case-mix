  No full text has been read. No data have been extracted, including Pass 1 (design) and Pass 2

CONSEQUENCE
  The registration will predate full-text eligibility assessment, all extraction and all analysis.
  It will not predate the search or the title/abstract screening, and the paper will not claim that
  it does. Running a search and screening abstracts gives limited opportunity to bias a pre-specified
  meta-regression, because neither step involves the outcome data. Abstracts that state a cut-off
  are the exception: they reveal the outcome. That exposure is already disclosed in SEARCH-LOG.txt
  and is handled by requiring a blinded human Pass 1.

ACTION
  1. Register protocol v1.0 on OSF without changes, with this deviation note attached.
  2. Pause full-text review until the registration exists.
  3. Report this deviation in the Methods section and in the PRISMA flow notes.
